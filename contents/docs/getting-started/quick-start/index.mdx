## Get Started with Acosta AI

Follow these steps to create your account and start chatting.

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Visit heyacosta.com">
    Open your browser and go to [heyacosta.com](https://heyacosta.com). You'll land on the Acosta AI homepage.
  </StepItem>

  <StepItem title="Create an account">
    Click **Sign Up** and enter your email address and a password.

    <Note title="Age requirement">
      You must be at least **13 years old** to create an account. If you're under 13, you'll need permission from a parent or guardian before signing up.
    </Note>
  </StepItem>

  <StepItem title="Verify your email">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from Acosta AI and click the confirmation link. This activates your account.
  </StepItem>

  <StepItem title="Sign in">
    Return to [heyacosta.com](https://heyacosta.com) and sign in with your email and password.
  </StepItem>

  <StepItem title="Start chatting">
    Type your first message in the chat box and press Enter. Acosta AI will respond instantly, using the best model variant for your request.
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## What's next?

Once you're signed in, you can:

- Ask questions, get help with writing, or explore ideas
- Upload images for visual analysis (Acosta 1 Vision)
- Use voice input for hands-free interaction (Acosta 1 Voice)
- Tackle complex problems that need deeper reasoning (Acosta 1 Think)

Acosta AI handles model selection automatically — just chat naturally.

<Note title="Tip" type="success">
  You don't need to specify which model variant to use. Smart routing picks the right one based on your message.
</Note>
