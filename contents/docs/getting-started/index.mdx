## What is Acosta AI?

**Acosta AI** is an intelligent AI assistant built by **Lachlan Thurlow** under the **Transnology** brand. It's designed to help everyday users and professionals think, write, and get things done — faster and smarter.

Available at [heyacosta.com](https://heyacosta.com), Acosta AI is powered by the **Acosta 1** model system — a suite of purpose-built AI model variants with smart routing that selects the right model for every task automatically.

## Who is Acosta AI for?

Acosta AI is built for anyone who wants a capable, reliable AI assistant:

- **Students** – Research, writing, and study support
- **Professionals** – Drafting, summarising, and problem-solving
- **Developers** – Code assistance and technical questions
- **Everyday users** – Quick answers, creative help, and more

## What makes it different?

### The Acosta 1 Model System

Acosta AI is powered by a suite of model variants under the **Acosta 1** family, each optimised for a specific purpose:

| Variant | Purpose |
| ------- | ------- |
| **Acosta 1 Lite** | Fast, lightweight responses for everyday tasks |
| **Acosta 1 Think** | Deep reasoning for complex questions |
| **Acosta 1 Vision** | Image understanding and visual analysis |
| **Acosta 1 Voice** | Natural voice interaction |
| **Acosta 1 Live** | Real-time, streaming conversations |

Smart routing selects the best variant for each request automatically — you don't need to think about which model to use.

### Built for Trust

Acosta AI is a registered sole trader product (ABN registered) operating under the Transnology brand. Your conversations are handled through a secure backend proxy, and your account is protected with Firebase Authentication.
